Sound familiar

You pick it up without deciding to.

You open it to check one thing. Twenty minutes later you are three apps deep and you cannot remember what you came for.

Meanwhile the things you actually want to do, the gym, the project, the book, the people, keep getting pushed to tomorrow.

  • You reach for it the second you are bored, waiting, or stuck
  • It is the first thing you see in the morning and the last thing at night
  • You have deleted the apps before, and they came back
  • You know what you want to do. You just can't seem to get going.
The twist

You're not unmotivated.
You were motivated to pick up your phone dozens of times today.

The problem isn't a lack of drive. It is where your drive is going. Things that pay you back instantly tend to beat the things that pay you back slowly, every single evening.

"Be more disciplined" has not fixed that for most people. In this hour you'll see why, and what tends to work instead.
