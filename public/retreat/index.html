Why this exists

You already know what to do. You can't make yourself do it.

Every day is a feed. Your phone, the TV, the podcast in your ears, the next thing to scroll. You haven't had an unstimulated hour in years, so you've never actually heard the quiet thought that keeps trying to tell you what you want.

The Reset removes all of it for four days and sees what's left. Not a wellness weekend. Not a bro camp. A controlled experiment on yourself, with structure, hard mornings, and two people who've done the uncomfortable thing enough times to walk you through it.

The three rules

These aren't the fine print. They're the product.

If any of these make you want to close the tab, that's the reason to come.

01

Phones live in the box.

Collected Thursday at lights-out. Twenty minutes Saturday evening to text whoever needs to hear from you, then back in. Friday is a full blackout. Watches, laptops, and Kindles count. You get a paper notebook instead.

02

You train every morning.

6:30 wake, cold water, then a session scaled to you by a competitive bodybuilder and certified trainer. Nobody opts out. The physical proof of the mental point.

03

You cook and clean.

Everyone's on a kitchen crew once. Nobody is served. Partly because it keeps the price down, mostly because the best conversations of the weekend happen over dishes.

The arc

Thursday evening to Sunday afternoon.

Every day has the same skeleton: wake, cold, train, eat, one deep block, open afternoon, dinner, fire. What changes is what the deep block is for.

Questions

The things people ask before they apply.

What if there's an emergency and someone needs to reach me?

Will's and [HER NAME]'s phones stay on, on silent, the whole weekend. Before you arrive you give your emergency contact their numbers. If something real happens, you'll know within minutes. That's a better safety net than a phone in your pocket that you check 200 times a day.

I'm not in great shape. Can I do the mornings?

Yes. Every session has a scaled version, and [HER NAME]'s job is to find yours. The rule isn't "crush it," the rule is "show up and finish." A 45-year-old desk worker and a marathoner both finish Friday's hill. One of them just takes longer.

What's the food situation?

High protein, no sugar, no alcohol, no caffeine after 10am. [HER NAME] sets the macros, which means it's what a bodybuilder's prep week looks like. You'll survive. Everyone cooks once in a pair from a fixed menu. Vegetarian is handled; tell us about allergies on the application.

I've never done silence or breathwork. Is this going to be weird?

Friday afternoon will be uncomfortable. That discomfort is the content. You're not expected to have a mystical experience; you're expected to sit with your own head for a few hours and notice what comes up. The breathwork is guided by someone who does this for a living and you can stop any time.

Is this co-ed? Can I bring my partner?

Yes to both. It's co-led by a couple for a reason. Partners book a Suite together. Singles are the majority and that's fine; nobody's pairing anyone off.

I'm flying in. How does that work?

Fly into Austin-Bergstrom (AUS) and land before 2:30pm Thursday. Book your return after 5pm Sunday. We pick you up and drop you off; it's about 45 minutes each way. If you're driving from Austin, you'll get the address a week out.

What happens after I apply?

Will reads every application and calls you within 48 hours for a ten-minute conversation. If it's a fit, you get a deposit link. $500 holds the seat, the balance is due 30 days before. The deposit is non-refundable but transfers to the next retreat if life happens.
