How the four days go

Simple on purpose.

  • Thursday. Arrive, eat, get to know everyone. Phones go in a box at the end of the night.
  • Friday. Train in the morning. Then a full day of quiet: trails, water, a notebook. A guided breathwork session in the afternoon. Silence breaks at dinner.
  • Saturday. Train. Spend the morning on the things you've been putting off looking at, with a therapist-led group session in the afternoon.
  • Sunday. Train. Turn what came up into a plan: one goal, small daily actions, who holds you to it. Phones come back after lunch.
  • The 30 days after. A group chat and a weekly call with everyone from the house, so it sticks.

Every morning starts with training, scaled to you. Everyone cooks once. Food is simple and clean. There's no alcohol and there are no screens.

Questions

Things people ask.

What if someone needs to reach me?

Will's and Carsyn's phones stay on the whole time. You give your emergency contact their numbers before you arrive. If something real happens, you'll know in minutes.

I'm not in great shape. Is that a problem?

No. Every workout is scaled to the person. Showing up and finishing is the only requirement.

What's the food like?

Simple and clean: lots of protein, vegetables, no sugar, no alcohol. Everyone helps cook once. Tell us about allergies when you apply.

Can I bring my partner?

Yes. Note it on the form and we'll put you in a private room together.

I'm flying in. How does that work?

Fly into Austin (AUS) and land before 2:30pm Thursday; book your return after 5pm Sunday. We pick you up and drop you off.

How much is it?

There are a few room options at different prices. Will goes through them on the call so you can pick what fits.

What happens after I fill out the form?

Will texts you to confirm, you book a 15-minute call with him, and if it's a fit he sends you a link to hold your spot. No pressure on the call; it's mostly to make sure this is right for you.
