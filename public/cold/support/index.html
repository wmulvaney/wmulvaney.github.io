Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?
Open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Cold. Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date.

I bought Cold Pro on another phone.
On the paywall, tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I get a refund?
Purchases go through Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where is my journal stored?
Only on your phone. Deleting the app deletes it.

How do I delete my community account?
Email us your nickname and we will delete your account, posts and likes.

How do I report a post?
Tap the menu on any post and choose Report. You can also block the person who posted it.
