FAQ
Will this happen overnight?

No, it will not happen overnight. But you will start noticing improvements after your first few experiences, and in 90 days you can realistically expect a drastic change in your dating life. The part most guys do not expect: it is exciting and rewarding the whole way through.

I'm not good looking, and I'm obviously never going to date supermodels. Why would I do this?

There are biological realities to physical attraction, and I am not going to tell you otherwise. What surprised me is how much of the rest is in your control, especially as a man.

Confidence and self-assuredness carry more weight than most guys expect, and they are built through consistent action, not by deciding you have them. You are also in control of your physical attractiveness: training, hygiene, clothes that fit, and a life you actually like will change how you look and how you come across more than you would guess.

How do I know if this is right for me?
This is for you if
  • You want to go on more dates, or find a relationship, and what you are doing isn't getting you there
  • You see women you would like to talk to and don't
  • You get nervous around women you find attractive and want to be comfortable instead
  • You are tired of relying on dating apps
  • You want to be a better man in general, not just with dating
This is not for you if
  • You want scripts, routines, or tricks
  • You are looking for a way to get someone who has said no to change her mind
