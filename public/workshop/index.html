The idea

Attraction is mostly built between dates.

A lot of guys treat dating as a performance: the right text, the right line, the right move at the right time. When it doesn't work, they look for a better script.

I think the more useful question is what kind of man your dating habits are turning you into. Chasing replies, rearranging your week around someone who isn't sure about you, going quiet when you like someone because you are afraid of a no: none of that is a line problem. It is a discipline problem, and it tends to make you less comfortable, which shows.

The workshop covers the other direction: saying what you want, being fine hearing no, meeting women in real life, and keeping your own life moving while you date. None of it requires being a different person. It requires practice, and a plan simple enough to keep.

What we cover

The 2 hours

01
The habits that quietly make you less attractive
Seeking validation, over-investing early, waiting until you feel ready. Why they are so common, and what to do instead.
02
Your standards: what you do whether or not she responds
A short list of behaviors you control completely. Asking clearly, following through on plans, not chasing, and ending things kindly when they are not working.
03
Meeting women in real life
Why comfort comes from reps, not material. How I handle the hesitation before walking up to someone: breathe deeper and name it, "I am feeling resistance," then go anyway.
04
Your life outside of dating
Training, sleep, friends, and work you care about. Not as a trick to look impressive, but because a full week makes it much easier to date without needing any single outcome.
05
Build your 30-day plan, live
Using the prep doc you fill out before the workshop, you write your own plan on the call: your standards, your weekly reps, and how you will track them.
06
Q&A
Bring the question you actually want answered. The prep doc asks for it ahead of time so I can get to as many as possible.

Is this for you
This is for you if
  • You want a relationship, or to date well, and what you are doing isn't getting you there
  • You get nervous around women you find attractive and want to be comfortable instead
  • You tend to over-invest early, then feel stuck when it doesn't go anywhere
  • You would rather build habits than memorize lines
This is not for you if
  • You want scripts, routines, or tricks
  • You are looking for a way to get someone who has said no to change her mind
  • You are not willing to fill out a 20-minute prep doc
