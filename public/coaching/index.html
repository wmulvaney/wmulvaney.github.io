FAQ
Who is this for?

Men, mostly in their 20s and 30s, who know they're capable of more and are ready to put in the work. You don't need to have it figured out. You just need to want to change something.

Is the intro call actually free?

Yes. No card, no obligation.

Do I need to want help with everything on this page?

No. Some men come in for one thing. Others want to work on all of it. We start with what matters most to you.

Is this therapy?

No. I'm a coach, not a licensed therapist. Coaching is about where you are now and what you do next. If something comes up that a therapist is better suited for, I'll tell you.

What does coaching cost?

We'll go over it on the intro call if it looks like a fit. No pressure either way.
