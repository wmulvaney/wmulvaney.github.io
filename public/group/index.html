How I coach

Small actions, done every week, for months.

Most people know roughly what they should be doing. The gap is doing it consistently once the first burst of motivation wears off, usually around week three.

My approach is simple. We define what you want, break it into small actions you can do this week, and track whether you did them. Every week I look at what happened and adjust the plan. The group calls and daily check-ins keep you doing it when you would otherwise drift.

Questions
What happens after the first month?

Your membership continues at $199 a month. You can cancel anytime before the next billing date and you won't be charged again.

When are the calls?

Two live video calls a week, currently Sunday and Wednesday, Central time. If you can't make one, make the other. I send the schedule in your welcome email.

Do I need to be in Austin?

No. The calls and the app are all online, so you can join from anywhere.

How much time does it take?

One or two hour-long calls a week, plus a few minutes a day to check in. The rest of the time goes into the actual work on your plan.

How is this different from 1-on-1 coaching?

1-on-1 adds private calls with me, daily check-ins by voice or video, and in-person sessions if you are in Austin. The group gets the same weekly plan and feedback, with the calls shared.
