The apps

Dating apps suck. Need I say more?

The apps are designed to make money off of you and keep you coming back. On them, you are one of many desperate men hoping she will do you the favour of going on one date with you.

Meanwhile, you do not even know if she wants to date. She might just want a free dinner.

👆
A better way

Skip the line. Talk to her in real life.

1

You choose who you talk to.

Not whoever an algorithm decides to show you. The woman you noticed at the coffee shop, the grocery store, or on the trail.

2

She gets to see the real you.

Your energy, your humour, how you carry yourself. Not just your looks in six photos.

3

You stand out.

Almost no guy does this anymore. When you do, you are the only one.

4

It is completely free.

No subscriptions, no boosts, no premium tier.

5

You build real confidence.

Every conversation is proof you can do something hard. That confidence shows up everywhere in your life, and it is extremely attractive.

The real problem

You're scared. And guess what, that's normal.

Every guy is terrified of doing this, which is exactly what makes you stand out so much once you can.

I used to be terrified at the thought of talking to an attractive stranger. You just have to trust that a process that has worked before will work for you.

If you follow this process, you will actually do it.

Not read about it. Not think about it for another year. When you see a beautiful woman, you will walk up and talk to her.

  • You'll know what to say, and when to say it
  • You'll become "rejection-proof"
  • You'll be more confident and attractive
Show me

I did it. That means you can too.

When I first saw Carsyn, I thought she was way out of my league. I walked up and talked to her anyway, because I had built myself into a confident and comfortable man through real-life experiences, not books, podcasts or reels.

I was never a "natural". I was the guy who always chickened out. What I learned over time is that confidence is a skill you can build. I learned it the hard way so I could give you a clearer path, without all the trial and error.

No. No tricks, no routines, no lines. It's learning to walk up to a woman you're interested in and have an honest conversation.
