The apps

Dating apps suck. You already know that.

You swipe for an hour. You get a few matches. Half of them never reply, and the conversations that do start die after three messages.

You're being judged on six photos and a sentence, next to hundreds of other guys doing the exact same thing. That isn't a reflection of what you have to offer. It's just a bad way to meet people.

Walking up to her, sober, is the best way to meet a woman.

The real problem

The problem is you're scared. I used to be terrified.

For years I'd see a woman I wanted to talk to and walk right past her. I'd rehearse a sentence ten times, never say it, and replay it the whole way home.

When I finally got past that fear, I paid attention to what actually worked. Then I turned it into a system, so other guys can learn to actually do it instead of just thinking about it.

You will actually get through it.

Not read about it. Not think about it for another year. Walk up and talk to her.

  • You'll know what to say, and when to say it, without memorizing lines
  • You'll get rejected less
  • And when it does happen, you won't care nearly as much

The fear doesn't go away by waiting for it to go away. It goes away one conversation at a time, with someone in your corner who has been exactly where you are.
