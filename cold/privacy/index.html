Privacy Policy

Cold: Meet Women IRL, an iOS app published by Willpower Coaching LLC. Effective October 30, 2026.

The short version

Your journal stays on your phone. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use third-party analytics.

Stored only on your device

  • Your journal: approaches you log, places, notes, fear ratings, check-ins and session history.
  • Your progress through the levels, your weekly goal and your onboarding answers.

This data never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

The community feed

If you open the Social tab, the app creates an anonymous account (no email, no name, no phone number) so you can read and share posts. We store:

  • The nickname you choose.
  • Posts you share, posts you like, posts you report, and people you block.

Posts are visible to other users under your nickname. This data is stored with Supabase (US region). Reported posts are reviewed and may be hidden.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Apple. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription or lifetime unlock is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history, never your payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no ad trackers.
  • No selling or renting of data.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.

Deleting your data

Journal data: delete the app. Community data: email us from the Support page with your nickname and we will delete your account, posts and likes within 30 days.

Children

Cold is for adults 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.
